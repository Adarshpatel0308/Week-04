"Corporate" refers to a legal entity known as a "corporation," which is a business structure where a group of individuals are recognized by law as a separate entity from its owners, allowing them to conduct business, own assets, enter contracts, and incur liabilities under the company name, with the key feature being limited liability for shareholders, meaning their personal assets are protected from company debts; essentially, a corporation acts as an individual with legal rights and responsibilities, enabling complex financial transactions and facilitating business growth.
